Help

  1. Open the app on an iPhone to see the total magnetic field, three axes and trend in microteslas (µT). Tap Freeze to hold the display.
  2. To compare with nearby metal or a magnet, remove magnetic accessories and set a baseline away from the target. Keep the same angle and distance. For a wall scan, sweep back along the same path after a peak. Readings only give clues to shallow steel fasteners. Use a suitable tool to check a wall before drilling.
  3. To compare spots, set a baseline, then capture each spot while holding your phone steady. Settings → Import preset accepts a MAG1 code or a .magmeterpreset file. Set a fresh baseline when you measure.

One payment on the App Store includes all features, with no ads or subscriptions.

Privacy

The app collects or uploads no data. Sensor readings, presets and comparisons are processed and stored on your iPhone. Apple handles App Store review requests. You decide when to share an image or file and what service to send it through. Deleting the app removes its local data.

磁场读数 · 使用帮助

  1. 在 iPhone 上打开 App，即可查看以微特斯拉（µT）表示的磁场总值、三轴与趋势。点「冻结」保留当前画面。
  2. 找金属和磁铁前，先取下磁吸配件，在远离目标处设基线，保持相同姿势和距离。查找墙内钉子时，出现峰值后沿原路线回扫。读数只提示浅层钢钉或螺丝的线索。钻孔前请用合适的工具检查墙体。
  3. 进入点位对照，设基线后拿稳手机记录各点。「设置 → 导入预设」可读取 MAG1 口令或 .magmeterpreset 文件，测量前请设置本轮基线。
